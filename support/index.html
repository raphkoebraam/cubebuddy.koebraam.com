Cube Buddy Support

Need help or found a bug? Write to support@koebraam.com with your device, the iOS version and what you expected to happen. A screenshot or a screen recording of the cube helps most.

Frequently asked questions

Which cube does Cube Buddy teach?

The standard 3x3 cube, with the layer-by-layer beginner method: white cross, white corners, second layer, yellow cross, yellow edges, yellow corners in place, yellow corners oriented. Every stage is free. The app works with any 3x3 in the standard color scheme (white opposite yellow, red opposite orange, blue opposite green).

How do I read the moves?

The lessons use the standard notation: a letter names a face (R right, L left, U up, D down, F front, B back), a prime (R') turns it the other way, a 2 (R2) twice. Learn → Notation trainer teaches it with the cube: see a move and pick the turn, or perform it yourself by dragging.

The lesson's cube does not match mine.

Each step starts from a prepared state so the moves make sense on screen; your own cube will differ. To work on your cube instead, go to Play, scan it or paint it on the net, and use Solve: it walks the same stages on your actual state.

The scanner reads a color wrong.

Scan in even light without a strong color cast and keep the face flat to the camera. After a scan you can tap any sticker on the net and correct it. The app refuses a state that cannot exist on a real cube and says which piece is off.

How does the timer start?

Touch and hold the timing surface until it turns ready, then let go to start; tap to stop. Inspection (the 15 seconds of a competition) and the hold delay can be switched in the timer settings. +2 and DNF are one tap on the last solve.

Can I bring my solves from another timer?

Yes. Timer settings → Import solves reads a CSV file with one solve per row, the format Export solves writes; the imported solves join the current session. Export solves writes the current session as CSV for the other direction.

What does Premium add?

The speedcubing algorithm sets as guided lessons: F2L (41 cases), OLL (57) and PLL (21), each with the pieces lit up on the cube, left-handed mirrors and explanations, plus training scrambles for the timer that set up a case for you. The beginner method, Play, the scanner, Solve and the timer stay free.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Cube Buddy Premium is managed by the App Store: Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or the subscriptions page. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Restore Purchases on the Premium page brings a subscription back on any device signed in to your Apple Account.

Where is my data?

On your device. Cube Buddy has no account and no servers; see the privacy policy.
