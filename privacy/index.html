Cube Buddy Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Cube Buddy does not collect any data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own, and sends nothing about you to us or to anyone else. Everything the app knows, which lessons you finished and the solves you timed, stays on your device.

Lesson progress and solves

The stages and steps you completed, your notation trainer scores, your timer sessions with their solves, comments and scrambles are stored in a database on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data. If you move to a new device, the standard iOS backup and transfer carry it over like any other app data.

The camera

The scanner (Play → Scan) uses the camera to read the sticker colors of a physical cube. The frames are analyzed on the device while the scanner is open and are not stored, not uploaded and not shared. The app asks for camera access the first time you open the scanner; everything else works without it.

The coach

The lesson coach answers questions about a move with the on-device model of Apple Intelligence when your device offers it, and with built-in explanations otherwise. Your question is processed on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Things you choose to share

Two features produce files from your data, and each one only leaves your device when you pick a destination in the iOS share sheet.

  • Solve export (Timer → Export solves): a CSV file with the solves of the current session: times, penalties, scrambles, dates and comments.
  • Copied scrambles: a scramble you copy goes to the clipboard and nowhere else.

Subscriptions

Cube Buddy Premium is an auto-renewable subscription processed entirely by Apple through your App Store account. Cube Buddy has no access to your payment details.

Diagnostics

The app keeps a short log of its own activity on the device to help with support, and reads the performance reports iOS produces for every app (MetricKit). Neither is sent anywhere unless you share it with us yourself.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds iCloud sync or optional crash reporting), the change will be described here with a new effective date before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@koebraam.com.
