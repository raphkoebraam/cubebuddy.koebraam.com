Learn the cube on a cube you can turn, then time your solves.

Cube Buddy teaches the beginner method for the 3x3 cube, stage by stage, on an interactive 3D cube: the pieces that matter light up, every move plays on screen, and you can step through at your pace. Scan the cube on your desk and let the app walk you out of any scramble. When you can solve it, the timer is ready.

Free on iPhone and iPad (iOS 26 or later). No account, no ads, nothing leaves your device.

Learn

Every move, shown on the cube

Seven stages take you from the white cross to a solved cube. Each step names the pieces it moves and lights them up, then plays the moves on the cube, one at a time or in one go. Turn the cube yourself to check the result, and ask the built-in coach when a move does not make sense. Your progress is kept, so you continue where you stopped.

Play

A cube in your pocket

Turn any layer with a drag, orbit the cube, undo a move. Scramble it the way a competition does, or set it to match the cube on your desk: paint the stickers on the net, or point the camera at it. Then let Solve take you out of the scramble, stage by stage, with the same explanations as the lessons.

Timer

Time your solves

Hold to start, tap to stop. WCA scrambles with a preview, inspection with voice alerts, +2 and DNF, comments, sessions for every cube or method. Averages of 5, 12 and 100, personal bests, a trend chart and a daily scramble to compare with yourself. Export your solves as CSV, or import the ones you already have.

Beginner method

Free, start to finish

The layer-by-layer method in seven stages, with spotlights, step-by-step playback and a coach that explains the why. Nothing of it is behind a purchase.

Notation

Learn to read algorithms

A trainer for the move notation: see a move, pick the turn, or perform it on the cube yourself. Three difficulty tiers.

Scanner

Your cube, in the app

Point the camera at each face and the app reads the stickers. Fix a sticker by hand if the light was bad; the app tells you when a state cannot exist.

Solve

Out of any scramble

From the scanned or painted state, Solve walks you through the beginner method with the pieces highlighted, until the cube is done.

Accessibility

Made for everyone

VoiceOver reads the cube, the scrambles and the lessons; Dynamic Type throughout; the timer works with a single tap.

Premium

Speedcubing sets as lessons

F2L, OLL and PLL, every case as a guided lesson with the pieces lit up, left-handed mirrors, and training scrambles for the timer. A subscription, monthly or yearly.

Private by design

No account with Cube Buddy, no analytics, no ads, no servers. Your lessons' progress and your solves stay on your device. The camera is used for the scanner only, and the pictures never leave the phone.
